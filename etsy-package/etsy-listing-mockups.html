Privacy & Parental Controls

Built for kids first. No sign-in, no camera, no mystery cloud.
You hold the PIN — you see every conversation.
🛡️ COPPA-friendly by design
🚫

No Sign-In

No account, no email, no login screen. Open the page and chat — nothing phones home.

🔐

PIN-Locked Settings

Parents set a 4-digit PIN. Kids can chat; only grown-ups can change the AI, voice, or timer.

⏱️

Usage Timer

Set a minutes-per-session cap. The robot gently wraps up when time’s running out.

🧠

AI Is Optional

Use offline phrase packs for a fully local experience, or bring your own API key for smart chat.

📜

Review History

Up to 20 sessions saved on-device. Read what your kid asked — never leaves the laptop.

💾

100% Local

No telemetry, no analytics, no cloud storage. Everything lives in your browser and your disk.
