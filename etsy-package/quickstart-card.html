Talking Robot

AI Voice Companion for Kids (ages 5–12)
No install • No account • 100% local • 5-minute setup
1
🌐

Open the App

Double-click index_kids.html in Chrome or Edge. No server, no install. Works offline as a PWA.

2
🎭

Name & Pick Personality

Give your robot a name, then choose one of 4 personalities — each with its own voice and vibe.

3
🎤

Click the Mic & Say Hi

Tap the big mic button and start talking — or type in the box if you prefer. Your robot replies out loud with feelings on its face.

4
🏴‍☠️

Swap Personalities

Try "be a pirate!" or "be a space explorer!" mid-chat. The voice, face and style all change on the fly.

5
📡

Optional: Pair a micro:bit V2

Got a BBC micro:bit V2? Flash the firmware via the in-app QR code to makecode.microbit.org, then hit Connect in settings. The robot’s face mirrors live onto the 5×5 LED face. Totally optional — the app is fully standalone without one.

Good to Know
🧠 Needs an API key for AI chatPaste your own OpenAI API key in Settings to unlock free-form chat. Your key stays on your device.
🔌 Offline demo modeNo key? No problem. Offline phrase packs give every personality a bank of replies — perfect for classrooms or plane rides.
🛡️ Parental controlsPIN-lock settings, toggle AI on/off, set a usage timer, review conversation history (up to 20 sessions, stored locally).
🎨 Two themesKid-friendly light theme by default, dark theme one click away. Dyslexia-friendly fonts & full keyboard nav.
