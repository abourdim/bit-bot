5 Listing FAQ (add inside the description or in a separate section)

Q: Do I need a micro:bit at all?

No — the micro:bit is completely optional. Talking Robot works standalone in any modern browser. Pairing a BBC micro:bit V2 only adds the face mirror on its 5×5 LED matrix.

Q: Will this work on my iPad?

Chat and typing work. Voice input (speech recognition) and optional micro:bit pairing do NOT work on iOS/Safari. Use a laptop, Chromebook, or Android device with Chrome or Edge.

Q: Does it use my OpenAI API key?

Yes, if you want AI mode. You paste your own key into the app (stored locally in your browser, never sent to us). If you'd rather skip the key, offline demo mode uses built-in phrase packs.

Q: What if my kid says something inappropriate, or the robot does?

Inputs and responses are filtered. Every conversation is saved locally (up to 20 sessions) so a parent can read exactly what was said. PIN-locked settings let you disable AI entirely and stick to offline phrase packs.

Q: Can I use this in my classroom of 30 students?

Yes — single teacher, one school, unlimited students. For multi-teacher / district licenses, message me.

Q: Is it safe for kids under 13 (COPPA)?

Yes. Runs locally — no accounts, no sign-ins, no telemetry. Nothing leaves the device except optional AI requests to OpenAI with your own key (and you can turn that off).

Q: Do you offer refunds?

Etsy doesn't allow refunds on instant digital downloads, so please try the live demo first. If it doesn't work as described on a supported browser, message me within 14 days and I'll make it right.

Q: Will it get updates?

Free updates within the v2.x line, plus bug-fix updates for 12 months — message me with your Etsy order number.
