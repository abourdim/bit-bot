Long paragraph

bit-bot is a browser-based AI chat companion designed for the classroom: 12 named personalities (pirate, professor, detective, chef, scientist, comic, bard, and more), each with its own system prompt, voice and visual identity. Traditional classroom AI forces teachers through enterprise accounts, data-processing agreements, and telemetry dashboards. bit-bot flattens that journey: paste an API key from any of six providers (Groq, Gemini, Cohere, Mistral, OpenRouter — or offline Demo mode), pick a personality, and speak or type. The answer comes back out loud. Keys live in the browser, never on a server. Parental controls include PIN-locked settings, a "Demo-only" lock for first minutes of class, and a session timer with auto-logout. Fully trilingual (EN/FR/AR with full RTL), PWA-installable, offline-capable in Demo mode, and COPPA/GDPR-friendly by design. One of the few Etsy AI tools built for education-sector procurement from day one.
