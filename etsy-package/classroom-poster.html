Meet Your New
Robot Friend!

Talking Robot • Classroom Guide

1
🌐

Open the App

Double-click index_kids.html in Chrome. No install, no login, no waiting. The robot wakes up and smiles at you!

2
🎭

Name Your Robot & Pick a Personality

Give your robot a name, then choose Playful, Helpful, Pirate, or Space Explorer. Each one talks in its own voice!

3
🎤

Say Hello!

Click the mic button and talk to your robot — or type if you prefer. Ask a question, tell a joke, share your day.

4
🔊

Listen Back

Your robot replies out loud with feelings on its face. Try each personality — a Pirate sounds very different from a Space Explorer!

5
✨

Optional Superpower

Got a BBC micro:bit V2? Pair it over Bluetooth and watch the robot’s face light up on the 5×5 LED grid in real time!

Curiosity makes the best friends — even robot ones.

Be kind, ask questions, and tell your robot something cool today.
